# 🧑‍💻 Get help with a custom task

{% hint style="info" %}
#### What support is included with my Mechanic subscription?

Our support team assists with **platform issues** and issues with tasks from the [Mechanic task library](https://tasks.mechanic.dev/). For these topics, contact us at [team@usemechanic.com](mailto:team@usemechanic.com).

For custom tasks not included in our task library, our support service is limited to Mechanic platform support. See the options below for getting help with custom tasks.
{% endhint %}

## Where the line sits

**Platform support** means how Mechanic itself behaves. We're glad to answer a specific question about a platform feature, even when you're asking it for a custom project:

* how a feature works: events, webhooks, actions, retries, filters, the cache, options and secrets
* what Mechanic did: what a run, an action or an error shows, and whether something on our side went wrong
* limits and policies: rate limits, email sending, data retention
* tasks from the [task library](https://tasks.mechanic.dev/), including fixing them when they break

**Custom work** means anything about your own task or integration as a whole. That's beyond what our support covers:

* designing how a system built on Mechanic should fit together (its architecture, its guarantees, how it handles failure)
* writing, reviewing, debugging or validating custom task code, including AI-generated code
* confirming that a custom integration does what your project needs, end to end

When a question is a bit of both, we'll answer the platform part and say plainly which part is custom. The [Slack community](resources/slack.md) and the [partner directory](https://partners.mechanic.dev/) are where the custom part goes best, and the platform facts we've given you are good to bring along.

## Getting help with custom tasks

### Slack Community

* **Community Support:** We encourage you to join our Slack community, where you can seek advice and share experiences with other Mechanic developers. Often, community members can offer insights or solutions based on their experiences.
* **Joining the Community:** You can join our Slack community through the following link: [Mechanic Slack Community](https://slack.mechanic.dev/).

### Hiring a Developer

* **Partner Directory:** We recommend hiring a developer if the issue requires more in-depth technical expertise. Our partner directory lists qualified developers familiar with Mechanic task development.
* **Finding a Developer:** Visit our partner directory at [partners.mechanic.dev](https://partners.mechanic.dev/) to find a developer who can customize or optimize your task. If you want to be matched with a suitable developer, use our [matchmaking service](https://partners.mechanic.dev/matchmaking).
